Bluetooth quick steps 1. Plug the JDY-16 module in (after uploading) and switch the car on.
2. Do not pair it in Windows settings, just have Bluetooth turned on.
3. Close the phone app, only one device can connect at a time.
4. Choose Bluetooth, click Connect, pick "CD PRINTS CAR" (or JDY-16 before reboot), click Pair.
5. Press a button. The log should show a reply like "-> FORWARD".
